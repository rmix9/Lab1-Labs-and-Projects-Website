Aruba

Aruba is the "A" of the ABC islands and it known as the happy island in the Caribbean. There are crystal clear waters and endless possibilities when you visit here. Besides the tourist attractions, Aruba's cultural heritage is very special, as the locals have many stories and traditions that have been carried on for quite some time.

Learn more about Aruba here!

Bonaire

Bonaire is the "B" of the ABC islands and the smallest of the four islands in this website. Bonaire, like the other islands, offers beautiful beach and shopping opportunities for tourists to access. The salt flats on the south side of the island are another popular tourist spot as it is one of the most unique things I have ever seen. Bonaire is also a popular place for windsurfing, among other water sports.

Learn more about Bonaire here!

Curaçao

Curaçao is the "C" of the ABC islands and is the largest of the 3 ABCs by land area and by population. Like this image above shows, Curacao has a vibrant and colorful culture, which is often an appealing trait of the island. The people of Curacao are very into sports like soccer and baseball. Just recently, Curacao launched its new professional baseball league. Curacao also recently appeared in the 2026 FIFA World Cup, sparking even more tourism on the island.

Learn more about Curaçao here!

Dominican Republic

The Dominican Republic is the only one of the four destinations listed that is not a part of the ABC islands, rather the DR shares an island with the country of Haiti. It is also the biggest nation listed and the second biggest in the Caribbean, at 48,671km2. The beaches residing on this island rival some of the best in the Caribbean. The sports culture on this island is also huge, mainly with baseball as the DR have some of the best players in the world and regularly dominate on the international stage. If you're looking to catch a game, this is the place to do it.

Learn more about the Dominican Republic here!
